`ca19ee087b601182e872235e58e6bf367f4bb21879226dfe3cc96f1c99624f61`.

## Implementation boundaries

Timed statue/potion effects, Stopwatch pause/resume, training-pass activation,
entry controls and entitlement gates are implemented using native packets.
Definitions are seeded from `internal/assetsql/timed_items_defaults.csv` into
structured asset tables. Duration and multiplier edits belong in SQL; runtime
never reloads the CSV. Both clients receive native status and expiry packets.

**Required compatibility policy (administrator clarification, 2026-11-09):**
Growth Statues and EXP potions are mutually exclusive in both directions.
An unexpired statue effect rejects EXP-potion use; an unexpired potion effect
rejects Growth Statue use. Rejection must consume no item, preserve the current
effect and its remaining time, and send a client-visible rejection. Do not
replace the existing effect or combine its multiplier with the other family.
Check this policy inside the same gameplay transaction that would consume the
item and activate/extend its effect. Once the existing effect expires, the
other family may be used. This rule is enforced by the activation transaction.

**Required training-expiry policy (administrator clarification, 2026-11-09):**
When a character's Training Island entitlement expires, that character must
not trigger a fight through enemy contact/chasing or by walking through an
enemy encounter/spawn area. Apply the eligibility check to both roaming-monster
contact and area encounter triggers; blocking only one path is insufficient.
Manual PK against monsters remains allowed with an expired entitlement, but
grants no battle rewards: no regular EXP, no flat Training Island EXP bonus,
no gold and no item drops. Do not confuse this manual monster challenge with
player-versus-player PK.

Expired characters must not bypass the reward restriction by joining another
character's fight. For mixed-entitlement parties, the imported native rules
text (`talk.45644.text`) separately confirms that a member without an active
pass receives neither EXP nor items even when an eligible teammate triggers
the encounter. Preserve per-member reward eligibility rather than suppressing
everyone's rewards. Enforce it before immediate per-kill item delivery as well
as final EXP/gold settlement, including participation pools. The server enforces this gate for immediate drops/captures, participation
pools and victory settlement.

Shared item effect definitions, durations, compatibility groups and training
destinations belong in the assets catalog. Remaining effect time and training
entitlements belong in gameplay SQL, with item consumption and entitlement
updates committed atomically. Runtime countdown/display state can be cached
according to DEVELOPMENT.md. The client should display authoritative receipts
and request authoritative teleports rather than changing EXP or maps itself.

Before claiming parity, obtain potion expiry data,
test other training-ticket IDs/durations and verify the remaining UI details
in mixed-client play. Offline/outside-map clocks follow the administrator policy
below; player/pet EXP coverage and same-family stacking have regression tests. The captures alone do not prove every cross-family
combination is incompatible; the administrator's explicit policy above governs
Gonline's required behavior in both directions.

## Training Island battle benefits

Administrator rules confirmed 2026-11-09 are seeded into the structured map
definitions for Training Island, its beach/cave/ocean/forest areas and Practice
Area (13050–13057 and 13059). The migration and repository seed include them;
runtime code has no hardcoded Training Island map-ID check.

At the end of each resolved PvE round, including a victorious final round,
living players and active pets recover 100 HP and 50 SP, capped at their maxima.
Defeated, withdrawn, absent and switching-in units are not revived/healed.
Recovery uses native stat-update steps, so Legacy playback and Gonline round
chains consume the same results. Escape/defeat endings do not grant recovery.

On victory, simple mode grants `scaled regular EXP + 200` to every participating
player and active pet, including those who never attacked or assisted. There is
no contribution requirement in simple mode. Participation mode creates one separate flat pool of
`200 * distinct contributing players/pets` for the whole battle, then divides
it with the existing inverse-level and full/half contribution weights. Idle
teammates are excluded. A contributor counts once across all kills; later full
participation replaces earlier half credit for this pool. The flat pool is not
increased by EXP rates, combo bonuses or the monster-level curve. Normal kill
EXP retains its existing calculation. Fleeing, defeat and PvP grant no training
EXP bonus. Timed consumable multipliers apply to the regular player/owned-pet reward
before adding the flat bonus. Paused boosts contribute no multiplier.

## Online clock policy

EXP boost time advances only while logged in. Training time advances only while
logged in inside maps flagged as Training Areas. Logout and reconnect preserve
remaining seconds. Stopwatch pauses/resumes the EXP clock and its multiplier;
training time continues independently. Counts are buffered and checkpointed with
recoverable session state; item activation and consumption commit together in SQL.

Training Voucher 34181 grants six hours, matching its stored and English
item descriptions. Training Fare 34258 grants one hour, matching the updated
Chinese description and stored description. Training Ticket 34253 specifies no
duration in either language or the stored description; the administrator has
confirmed it should grant six hours as well.

Native Stopwatch confirmation callbacks `LAB_00441904` and `LAB_0018f8e0`
send AC23:110 with the bag slot to pause and slot zero to resume. The AC23:204
receiver stores a separate pause boolean (initial mode 1, changed mode 2).
Resuming consumes no item. Explicit monster challenges use AC11:2:3 with the
NPC template and nonzero click ID (`FUN_0038d1ac`), validated against the actual
visible monster; automatic contact paths retain their entitlement checks.
